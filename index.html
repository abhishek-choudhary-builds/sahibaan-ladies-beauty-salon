Why people keep coming back

Guests who have been going for twenty-five years.

Across seven hundred reviews, one pattern stands out more than any service list. Guests do not describe a first visit. They describe a habit — sometimes three decades old.

Business listings put the salon's opening at 1992. The reviews agree. A regular client writing four years ago described herself as a customer of twenty-nine years; another, two years before that, of twenty-five. One guest recalled visiting with her mother since 1996, and still going.

What guests mention most

Makeover comes up more than anything else.

These are the phrases Google groups from the salon's 718 reviews. Makeover leads the list by a wide margin — five times the next service — and the two strongest themes after it are the team's temperament rather than any treatment.

The salon's own business listing describes it as a one-stop destination for skin and hair care, naming de-tan, deep cleansing, nourishing, brightening and hydrating facials, regular and fashion hair colour, hair spa, anti-dandruff treatment, straightening and smoothening, keratin, and pre-bridal and bridal work.

Questions

Good to know before you go.

How long has Sahibaan been open?

Business listings on Justdial and pro.makeup both give 1992 as the year it was established, and the salon's own listing describes it as having more than 25 years in the industry. The reviews support this: guests describe being customers for 15, 18, 20, 25 and even 29 years.

Is it a ladies-only salon?

Yes. The salon's name and its business listings describe it as catering exclusively to women and girls.

What are the opening hours?

Google shows the salon opening at 11:30 am. A business directory last updated in January 2024 listed 11:00 am to 8:00 pm, Monday to Saturday, with Sundays closed — but its opening time is already out of date, so we have not published a closing time. Please call on +91 79809 05657 to confirm.

How do I pay?

Google lists payment as cash and Google Pay only. Credit and debit cards are not accepted, so please bring cash.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Google lists appointments as required.

Do they run beautician courses?

One guest describes spending five months on a professional beautician course here, and the owner replied thanking her and calling her their student. A separate business listing also tags the salon for beauty training. Please call to ask whether places are currently available.

Is the salon large?

No — and it is worth knowing before you go. This is a compact neighbourhood salon on a busy road, and two reviewers have noted the limited space. Booking ahead is sensible, particularly for makeover and bridal work.

Where exactly is it, and how do I get there?

22/1, Bidhan Sarani Road, opposite Srimani Market, beside Reliance Jio Store, Simla, Machuabazar, Kolkata 700006 — Plus code H9M8+GQ.
